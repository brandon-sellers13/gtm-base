01
Visual grammar

1. The Working Atlas

Description: Keep the warm paper and two-type foundation, then replace soft SaaS polish with atlas plates, coordinate margins, strong rules, metadata rails, high contrast, and semantic inks. Every object follows a governed-record order: name, owner or source, version and date, state, then content.

Basis
Direct: The current system is warm and typography-led, but the gradient and cards are generic and faint text has a known contrast problem. External: Atlases organize dense evidence through hierarchy, legends, and annotation.
Rationale
A map-based visual grammar matches a product that joins multiple company surfaces into one navigable base.
Downsides
Precision can become cold or overly technical. Editorial layouts need more whitespace and human texture than product screens.

02
Identity primitive

2. The Registered Join

Description: Replace the gradient user icon with a compact registration mark in which four context surfaces align at one shared coordinate or seam. The geometry can separate, drift, propose, and realign in product states and motion.

Basis
Direct: Map, layers, joins, drift, files, and currentness are native GTM Base concepts. External: Cartographic registration marks align independently produced layers.
Rationale
The product operation becomes the recognition device, which is more defensible than abstract AI symbolism.
Downsides
Four inputs can become visually busy at very small sizes. The final mark needs optical testing at 16, 24, and 32 pixels.
Confidence
91%
Complexity
Medium
Role
Primary mark and motion seed
03
Family architecture

3. The Operating Record

Description: GTM Base is the maintained system, MICW is its public implementation record, and Brandon Sellers is the accountable builder and editor. A compact provenance signature with module, version, effective date, and state links the family more honestly than matching logos.

Basis
Direct: GTM Base maintains versioned, approvable context. MICW ships a working module per issue. External: Stripe Press shows that editorial can own a distinct premise while retaining parent attribution.
Rationale
Every issue becomes checkable product evidence instead of content marketing adjacent to the product.
Downsides
The relationship needs disciplined naming. Brandon should sign the work without becoming a visual mascot or competing master brand.

93%

04
Evidence language

4. The Evidence Legend

Description: Establish one visual syntax for sources, joins, freshness, AI-produced work, human approval, drift, and corrections. Product screens, documentation, issues, and social cards all use the same legend and proof rail.

Basis
Direct: Joined surfaces and traceable sources explain the product's value. External: Cartography makes complex representations trustworthy through shared legends.
Rationale
Repeated evidence syntax can become the identity itself while teaching people how to evaluate an output.
Downsides
The legend must stay small. Too many states or icons will create a compliance manual instead of a usable language.
Confidence
89%
Complexity
Medium
Role
Diagram and state system
05
Evidence language

5. The Correction Is the Cover

Description: The recurring proof object shows what the system believed, what new evidence conflicted, what changed, who approved it, and which current version resulted. GTM Base uses it operationally, while MICW publishes the most instructive sanitized correction as the issue cover.

Basis
Direct: The decisive AI-native test is whether the system can be wrong and know it. MICW promises honest accounts of what broke. External: Cloudflare Radar turns real operational data into public brand objects.
Rationale
Real corrections are more credible and harder to copy than abstract AI illustrations or invented dashboards.
Downsides
Some weeks will not produce a visually compelling correction. The system needs a fallback evidence object, usually the context map or release diff.
Confidence
90%
Complexity
Low
Role

06
Surface expression

6. One Network, Four Reading Speeds

Description: Treat product, documentation, MICW, and social as different map scales and reading speeds. They share nouns, type roles, semantic colors, join geometry, and evidence syntax, but product is dense, docs procedural, MICW narrative, and social compressed.

Basis
Direct: The identity must work across surfaces with genuinely different jobs. External: GitHub, IBM, and transit systems retain one network while adapting expression to context and scale.
Rationale
MICW can feel editorial and GTM Base operational without splitting into unrelated brands.
Downsides
Requires examples, not only token documentation. Otherwise teams will collapse back to one generic card system.
Confidence
88%
Complexity
Medium
Role
Cross-surface governance

Rejection Summary

Every cut either claimed more than the product has earned, duplicated a stronger direction, or pushed into premature automation.

CandidateReason rejected or absorbed
The Maintained StandardRejected. “Standard” implies authority GTM Base has not earned, and generic status labels will not create recognition alone.
The Accession MarkAbsorbed into The Operating Record. Module codes and editions belong in the provenance signature, not as a second identity system.
The Context LedgerAbsorbed into The Working Atlas as the construction rule for every object.
The Brand CompilerDeferred. Generating outward assets from approved metadata is a strong future behavior, but the required structured fields do not yet exist.
Editioned InstrumentsAbsorbed into the governed-record grammar. Its claim that the catalog reveals every missing operating job was not supported.
Approval as the primary markCut as too narrow. Approval is one important state inside the Evidence Legend, not the whole product identity.
Generic editorial imprintCut as incomplete. The imprint analogy explains hierarchy but does not create an ownable visual or evidence system.
