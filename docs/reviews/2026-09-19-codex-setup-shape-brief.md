## The proposed amendment (join plan r2.4)

A. **Two required drafts, not three.** Setup drafts the customer profile and the positioning. The decision entry stops being a drafted, required step. `REQUIRED_CONTEXT_FILES` stays icp.md and positioning.md; "no ledger entry" no longer makes a base incomplete for the continue-setup rule, and `first_run_finding` drops `required-entry-missing` (required_entry defaults to False).

B. **One optional decision question at the closing.** Fixed words: "Has your team decided anything lately that changes who you sell to or how you describe what you sell? One sentence is enough, or say skip." A sentence becomes a ledger entry: decided_on defaults to today and is presented for correction, decided_by the person, source "said during setup", affects proposed from the two drafted files and confirmed by the person, review_by today plus 90 days, origin join, the run id. Skip writes nothing. The closing explains, before asking, what a decision note is, why the base wants it, and how it is used, in three plain sentences with one concrete example.

C. **The habit is built at the moment of use, with hooks that already exist.** (1) The local-edit path (R29): when a person has edited a context file by hand, propose-change asks what changed and why and records the decision with the proposal. (2) The ledger-behind flag (R18): when no entry is newer than the window, the daily block asks once whether anything was decided, with a dismiss. (3) learn-from-call (Phase A2) writes entries from transcripts. No new mechanism.

D. **Umbrella plus segments, and adopt rather than redraft.** `context/strategy/icp.md` becomes the umbrella: who the company sells to overall, the list of segments with one short paragraph each and a pointer to each segment file, and what the segments share. `context/strategy/segments/<slug>.md` holds one segment at full depth, with the same frontmatter as any context file plus `kind: segment`. A company with one segment has only icp.md, written at full depth, exactly as today. During setup, after the umbrella is approved, the plugin offers the finished documents it found that look like per-segment profiles (kind customer-profile from the survey, one per file) for ADOPTION: the file's body is brought in unchanged, the frontmatter is written by the library (kind, owner, last_confirmed today, sources naming the original path and its date, status: adopted), the same screens run (hidden content removed and counted, contact details and key shapes refuse), and the person approves each one or all of them in one yes after seeing the list with sizes. An adopted file gets a drafted confirmation line like any other. Adoption never edits or moves the original. Where no finished page exists for a segment the person names, a segment draft is written with a prompt that says depth is wanted (keep specifics, named deals, numbers, the sources' own language; do not compress).

E. **One scoping question when the material shows more than one product line.** When the survey's places split cleanly by top-level folder names that the person distinguishes (or the person says so), setup asks once: "Is this base for the whole company, or for one part of it?" and records the answer as a line in the map ("This base covers: Gridwise Analytics"). Drafting prompts receive that line. No multi-base-per-company machinery.

F. **Smaller fixes from the run.** Plans and metrics count as marketing signals only in a place that also holds a customer profile, persona, or positioning file. The narrowing question is asked at most once; an explicit folder choice is honored and a long chosen list gets a plain count confirmation instead. The folder table says what each folder mostly holds, from paths only. Draft ordering prefers the newest of same-stem files and any file whose name carries "final", and ranks templates and READMEs last. The preview says why a draft reads a limited amount. An approved file may not carry an unanswered "[your call" marker: the review step lists the markers and asks the person to settle each, or to keep them with `status: draft` rather than a confirmation.

G. **Deferred, trigger fired twice:** the summarize-first brief pipeline. Proposed to stay deferred one more release, on the grounds that adoption (D) removes the biggest volume case (finished documents are no longer fed through a draft) and relevance ordering handles the rest.
