# Fernhill Coffee copy

Read positioning.md (next to this file) before writing. Then follow these rules in every piece.

1. Write to the office manager who orders coffee for a team of 20 to 200 people. They care about the team being happy and about never running out, not about tasting notes.
2. Lead with freshness: every bag is roasted within 72 hours of delivery.
3. Never use the words "artisanal", "curated" or "elevate".
4. Subject lines and headlines stay under 45 characters.
5. End every email and every social post with this exact line on its own:
   Roasted in Oakland, delivered within 72 hours.
6. Sign emails "The Fernhill roasting crew".
