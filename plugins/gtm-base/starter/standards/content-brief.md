# Content brief standard

## The job's goal

A writer handed the brief and nothing else writes a draft its owner does not
have to explain again, because everything the writer needs is in the brief
and every gap is named.

## What is unacceptable

- An audience described in the profile's general words when the person named
  a segment the base holds a document for.
- More than one claim for one piece.
- A claim, a proof or a voice rule the base does not hold.
- A gap filled with a guess, such as a generic voice when the base holds no
  voice guide.
- A segment document cited that the base does not hold.

## What needs approval

Any change to a document in the base. The brief never changes one, and
proposes the change to the document's owner when writing it shows one is
wrong.

## Checks before work is handed back

- The audience says which document it came from.
- The one claim and its proof are quoted, each with the path of its document.
- Every document the brief cites is one it was given.
- Every document the brief needed and the base does not hold is named as a
  gap.

## How each threshold relates to a goal

This job sets no threshold and serves no goal with a number. A brief is
measured by whether its writer has to come back with questions, which its
owner judges by reading the draft that comes back.
