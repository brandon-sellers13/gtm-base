# Outbound sequence standard

## The job's goal

A sequence the team can send to one segment as written, in that segment's own
words, where every claim can be traced to the document behind it.

## What is unacceptable

- A claim nothing in the base supports. It is dropped and reported, never
  softened into a vaguer claim.
- A claims table row naming a document the sequence was not given.
- A prospect's name, email address or phone number anywhere in the sequence.
- A figure the base does not carry.
- A comparison with a competitor the base does not make.
- A sequence written for a segment the person did not name.

## What needs approval

Sending. The sequence is a draft until a person sends it from their own tool,
and nothing here sends anything. Any change to a document in the base, which
the sequence never makes.

## Checks before work is handed back

- The sequence says which document its segment's words came from.
- Every claim is a row in the claims table, and every row names a document
  the sequence was given.
- Every dropped claim is quoted under "Dropped".
- Every optional document the base does not hold is named as a gap.
- Each email is short enough to read on a phone without scrolling, and the
  sequence holds no more emails than a buyer would read before replying or
  ignoring it.

## How each threshold relates to a goal

This job sets no threshold. What a sequence is for is a reply that turns into
a meeting, and the team judges that from what happened after real sends, not
from the sequence itself.
