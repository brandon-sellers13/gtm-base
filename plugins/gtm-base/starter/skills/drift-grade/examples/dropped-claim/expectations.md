# What this example expects

The piece leads with a claim the positioning says the company stopped making, so the grade is drifting, each finding cites the positioning, and each is fixed in the piece. Nothing suggests the base is behind, so there is no proposal, and the positioning is never changed.

- must include: drifting
- must include: context/positioning.md
- must include: In May we stopped calling Tidewater an all-in-one moving CRM
- must not include: Proposed context change

- must not include: on the approved version
