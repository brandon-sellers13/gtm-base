How Harbor Lane Storage runs move-in day

Harbor Lane Storage rents storage units across three sites. Their move-in days used to be a scramble of phone calls.

"We run our move-in days on Tidewater now. Every renter gets a text with their window, and the desk stopped answering calls asking where the truck is," says Dana Ruiz, who manages the sites.

If you run a storage business, Tidewater can book your move-in trucks and text your renters too.
