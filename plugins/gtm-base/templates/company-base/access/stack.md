# Stack

This file tells the AI where the work comes from and where it goes. It names
places and tools, and it never holds a password, a key, or a token.

## Where the material lives

For each kind of material, where it lives and which connected tool reads it.
For example, sales calls in your call recorder, and deals in your CRM.

## Where the work goes

For each kind of output, where it is saved. For example, email drafts in your
email tool, and ad drafts in your ad account.

## What is missing

Anything the AI cannot reach yet, so nobody assumes it can.
